    <br>Toolkitty is a peer-to-peer (P2P), local-first application for desktop and mobile designed to help collectives, festivals, organizers, venues and community spaces by facilitating decentralized resource exchanging and event coordination in a collaborative calendar. We are building it for groups that value autonomy, resilience, and privacy in their organisational practices.
