  <br>We were the maintainers of Circles UBI until 2025, a protocol and open source project running a basic income on the Ethereum blockchain. Since we
  launched the project in 2020, Circles had become a wider network of collectives and initiatives building alternative economic systems for their local
  neighborhoods. The initial work has mostly been carried by the Bitspossessed developer collective and the <a
  href="https://e2c.how/case-studies/circlescoop/" target="_blank">Circles Cooperative in Berlin</a> . If you are interested
  in more information about Circles you can read the <a
